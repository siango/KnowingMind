# Contribution Points Policy V1

## Purpose

Contribution points recognize support to the KnowingMind community.

They are not a measurement of Buddhist merit, karma, holiness, spiritual attainment, moral worth, or religious rank.

Recommended public terminology:

Thai: แต้มเกื้อกูล / แต้มสนับสนุนชุมชน  
English: Contribution Points  
Simplified Chinese: 贡献积分

## Eligible contribution classes

- CODE
- CODE_REVIEW
- TRANSLATION
- TRANSLATION_REVIEW
- SOURCE_VERIFICATION
- DOCUMENTATION
- COMMUNITY_SUPPORT
- CONTENT_PRODUCTION
- COMPUTE_SUPPORT
- FINANCIAL_SUPPORT

## Event rule

Points may only be issued from an accepted/verified contribution event.

Examples:

PR_OPENED → no points by default  
PR_MERGED → eligible  
TRANSLATION_SUBMITTED → pending  
TRANSLATION_REVIEWED_ACCEPTED → eligible  
PAYMENT_CREATED → no points  
PAYMENT_VERIFIED_BY_WEBHOOK → eligible under policy

## Anti-gaming rules

- no points for raw commit count,
- no points for duplicate/spam issues,
- no self-awarded verification,
- reversals are possible for reverted/fraudulent events,
- quality and impact policy must be published,
- automation may calculate points but the source event must remain auditable.

## Financial support

Money must not map to claims such as "religious merit units."

Financial support can receive contribution recognition, badges, or transparent support tiers as community acknowledgements only.

## Authority separation

Points do not confer:
- Canon authority,
- doctrinal authority,
- moderation immunity,
- payment privileges,
- access to private user data.
