# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

Be respectful, patient, precise, and open to correction. Discuss ideas and
evidence without attacking people. Treat Buddhist traditions, practitioners,
reviewers, and source communities with care.

Unacceptable conduct includes harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing,
deliberate misrepresentation of sources, or publishing another person's
sensitive or private data.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository maintainers. Maintainers
may edit, remove, or reject contributions and may temporarily or permanently
restrict participation. Enforcement decisions should be documented without
unnecessarily exposing private information.
