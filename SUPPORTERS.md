# Supporters / ผู้สนับสนุน

Thank you to everyone who contributes time, knowledge, review, infrastructure, and financial support to KnowingMind / Dhamma Grounded AI.

## Financial support

Support is used for project operating costs such as:
- server and infrastructure expenses;
- AI/service usage required for development and operation;
- public project delivery and maintenance.

The current public support link is managed through Stripe and is linked from the project website and README.

## Public recognition policy

Supporter names are **not published by default**.

A supporter may be listed publicly only when:
1. the supporter explicitly opts in to public recognition;
2. the exact display name they want published is confirmed;
3. no payment identifiers, email addresses, billing details, transaction IDs, or private metadata are exposed.

Financial contribution does not grant:
- Canon authority;
- doctrinal authority;
- privileged access to private user data;
- control over project truth or verification.

## Other forms of support

We also recognize:
- code and documentation;
- review and testing;
- accessibility and localization;
- source/provenance work;
- infrastructure and operational assistance;
- community education and feedback.
